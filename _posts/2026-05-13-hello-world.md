I've been meaning to start a blog for a while.

Not because I have a grand writing plan or a niche to fill — I just think better when I write, and some things are worth putting down somewhere more permanent than a note on my phone.

I'll write about whatever I'm thinking about: data, research, things I've learned at work, places I've been, books and ideas that stuck with me. No fixed schedule, no theme. Just writing when I have something worth saying.

If something here resonates with you, feel free to reach out.

— Hrishav
